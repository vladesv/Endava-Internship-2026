TC-003: Verify that the progress bar reaches 100% and can be reset

Website: https://demoqa.com/progress-bar

Environment: Windows 11, Microsoft Edge

Description

Verify that the progress bar starts correctly, increases its percentage until reaching 100%, and can be reset to its initial state.

Preconditions

The user has opened the Progress Bar page, and the progress bar is in its initial state.

Test Data

No additional test data required.

Steps to Reproduce

1. Navigate to https://demoqa.com/progress-bar.
2. Locate the progress bar and the "Start" button.
3. Click the "Start" button.
4. Observe the progress bar increasing its percentage.
5. Wait until the progress bar reaches 100%.
6. Verify that the progress bar stops at 100%.
7. Click the "Reset" button.
8. Observe whether the progress bar returns to its initial state.

Expected Result

The progress bar should start increasing after clicking "Start", reach 100%, and stop automatically. After clicking "Reset", the progress bar should return to 0% and be ready to start again.
