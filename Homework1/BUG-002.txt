BUG-002: Left navigation bar text overflows and overlaps other menu elements

Website: https://demoqa.com/browser-windows

Environment: Windows 11, Microsoft Edge

Description

The left navigation bar displays text that overflows its designated menu area. The "Alerts, Frame & Windows" category label extends beyond its intended boundaries and overlaps with the "Browser Windows" submenu item, causing the navigation menu to appear incorrectly formatted.

Steps to Reproduce

1. Navigate to https://demoqa.com/browser-windows.
2. Locate the left navigation bar.
3. Expand the "Alerts, Frame & Windows" category.
4. Observe the positioning of the category label and its submenu items.
5. Notice that the text overlaps with the "Browser Windows" submenu item.

Expected Result

The navigation bar should display all category labels and submenu items within their designated boundaries, without any text overlapping or overflowing into other elements.

Actual Result

The "Alerts, Frame & Windows" category label overflows its container and overlaps with the "Browser Windows" submenu item, making the navigation bar appear cluttered and difficult to read.

Impact

This issue negatively affects the website's visual appearance and usability. Overlapping text makes navigation more difficult and may confuse users when trying to identify or select menu options.
